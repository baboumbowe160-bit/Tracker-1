Privacy policy

Agent & Client Tracker. Last updated 4 October 2026.

Who we are

Agent & Client Tracker is provided by the app owner. Questions about your data: contact the app owner.

What the app stores

Your account: your email address and password. The password is handled by Google's sign-in service and is never visible to us.

Your details: the name or business name and the country you choose, and your settings, such as currency, colour theme and how you handle tips.

Your records: what you type into the app, such as sales, customer and agent names, phone numbers, amounts, the date and time of each transaction, the moment you recorded it, commissions, money you paid out, balances, notes and settings.

Your devices: so you can see which phones are signed in to your account, the app saves a short name for each phone (for example the phone model and browser) and when it was last used.

The app does not collect your location, your phone contacts, or advertising or tracking data, and it shows no adverts.

Where it is stored

Your records are always kept on your phone so the app works without internet. If you sign in, they are also stored in your private account on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a service run by Google. Google may store this data outside your country.

Who can see your records

Only you, when signed in. Security rules allow each account to read and change only its own records. As the operator of the online service, the app owner has technical administrator access to the database and will only use it to keep the service running or when required by law.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone, except Google as the service provider, or where the law requires it.

Details about your customers

If you record other people's details, such as your customers' names and phone numbers, you are responsible for having their permission and using them fairly.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your records are kept until you delete them or delete your account. You can delete your account and every record in it at any time:

In the app: Settings, then Your Account, then Delete my account and records.
On the web: delete your account here.

Deletion is permanent. Signing out also removes your records from that phone.

Security

Data travels between your phone and Google over encrypted connections. Keep your password private and do not reuse it elsewhere.

App lock: you can protect the app on your phone with a PIN and, if your phone supports it, your fingerprint or face. The PIN is kept only on that phone, in a scrambled form, and is never sent to us or to Google. Your fingerprint or face is handled by your phone and is never seen by the app. If you have an account, the app asks for your password again after the number of days you choose.

Your devices: you can remove any phone from your account. A removed phone is signed out and its copy of your records is erased.

Children

Agent & Client Tracker is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
